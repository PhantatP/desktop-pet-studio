# Privacy Policy for Desktop Pet Studio

*Effective date: October 11, 2026*

Desktop Pet Studio ("the app") is made by Yuriyaa. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: **the app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.**

## Information the app collects

None. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send any data over the internet.

## Information that stays on your device

To work, the app keeps some data **only on your own computer**:

- **Your pets:** their names, settings, and the sprite sheet images you import or create.
- **App settings:** pet size, walking speed, climbing, and whether the app starts with Windows.

This data is removed when you uninstall the app.

While running, the app reads the **position of your mouse cursor** and the **positions and sizes of open windows** so that pets can react to clicks and walk on top of windows. This is used only in the moment, and is never saved or sent anywhere.

## Sharing pets

When you choose **Export**, the app saves a pet (its name and images) to a `.deskpet` file at a location you pick. Nothing is shared unless you send that file to someone yourself. When you import a `.deskpet` file, its contents are stored on your device like any other pet.

## Third-party components

The app runs on Microsoft Edge WebView2, a part of Windows, and is distributed through the Microsoft Store. These are provided by Microsoft and covered by the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

## Children

The app does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new effective date.
